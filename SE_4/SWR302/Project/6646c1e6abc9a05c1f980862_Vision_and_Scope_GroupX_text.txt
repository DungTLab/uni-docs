Business Requirements
1.1. Background
The Background my team will have interface background is white and use the main image to create a highlight for the website and web above will have a function to help users manipulate the website easily. 
When put into use, the website will continuously update with the latest version to fix errors for users to give users the best experience.
1.2. Business Opportunity
Growing and trading coffee is a very "hot" business at the moment. We will find coffee shops sprouting on the streets like mushrooms. People often drink more coffee so the demand for coffee is huge. Many consumers, especially housewife and office workers, have a very high demand for convenience and quality. Our team can provide a websites sell fresh cafe with good-looking interface, but easy to manipulate, no cumbersome and also meet different search needs according to each interest. When people can place an order on the website, all the store's sales information will be quickly stored to help the sales staff save time and have time to focus on selling, which helps increase profit. The manager will be able to manage the cafe sold, reducing waste and avoid dishonest staff. No one wants to spend 1 to 2 hours to drive to the store only buy a bag of coffee, while just searching, selecting flavors, type of cafe, producer and finally a few clicks to confirm. 
The information is that you can have a bag of fresh beans coffee immediately, but it only takes 10 to 15 minutes to wait for the shipper to deliver.
1.3. Business Objectives
BO-1: Reduce coffee operating cost by 17% within 12 months following initial release.
BO-2: Increase average effective work time by 20 minutes per coffee-using employee per day
Within 6 months following initial release.

Success Metrics
SM-1: Due to the nature of the work, about 100 people will visit the website every day to place orders to save time.
SM-2: When there is a website, the store's product is known by many people and the number of visits also increases
SM-3: Most of the users give high rating to the website although the website has just been put to use recently
Vision Statement
For users, due to the nature of the work and some other issues, they can order conveniently without much effort. The website system selling beans coffee can meet this demand of them easily. Currently due to the developing society towards modernization the above order will help everyone who saves time and reduces unnecessary risks. The system will save people time on necessities, which is a big step forward for the promotion of the traditional beans coffee business.
Business Risks
RI-1: Too few customer might use the system, reducing the return on investment from the system development and the changes in coffee operating procedures. (Probability = 0.3; Impact = 9).
RI-2:The local delivery system may not meet the minimum delivery time, which means that customer would not always receive their order on time which reduces customer satisfaction with the website. (Probability = 0.5; Impact = 6).
Business Assumptions and Dependencies
AS-1: Systems with suitable user interfaces will be available to coffee staff to handle expected volumes ordered.
AS-2: Delivery personnel will deliver within the specified delivery time frames within 1 day of the requested delivery time.
DE-1: If processing a delivery via a shipper, the owner of the website must be able to communicate with that shipper bi-directionally.

3.3. Deployment Considerations
The web server software is required to be upgraded to the latest version. Any relevant infrastructure changes must be made at the time of the system upgrade. Develop Team has to use videos no more than five minutes in length to train users (staff, manager, owner) in the Internet-based versions of COW.
