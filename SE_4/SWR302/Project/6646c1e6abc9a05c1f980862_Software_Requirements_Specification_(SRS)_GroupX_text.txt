Introduction
Purpose 
This SRS describes the functional and nonfunctional requirements for software release 1.0 of the Coffee Ordering Websites (COW). This document is intended to be used by the members of the project team who will implement and verify the correct functioning of the system. Unless otherwise noted, all requirements specified here are committed for release 1.0.
Document Conventions
No special typographical conventions are used in this SRS.
Project Scope and Product Features
COW will allow customers to order coffee online from the Coffee Ordering Websites and be delivered and paid by a salesperson at home or purchased and paid for directly in the store. A detailed description is available in the Coffee Ordering Websites Scope and Vision Document [1], along with features scheduled for full or partial rollout in this release.

Overall Description
Product Perspective
Coffee Ordering Websites is a new software system that replaces the traditional way of buying in a customer's store by allowing viewing, ordering and home delivery. The context diagram in Figure 1 illustrates the external entities and the system interface for version 1.0.

Figure 1. Context diagram for release 1.0 of the Coffee Ordering Websites.
User Classes and Characteristics
Customer
Customers are those who buy goods directly at the store or order online from the store's website. An estimated 60% of orders come from online ordering through the website. The remaining 40% of orders come from direct in-store purchases.
Staff
The store employs about 5 employees, who sell directly at the store or take orders from COW, prepare products, pack for delivery. Tracking and updating order status will be done by staff. Most Store Staff will need training on how to use hardware and software for COW.
Manager
The Product Managers is a store employee who sets up and maintains a store's business products. Some products may not be available for delivery. Product Managers will need to periodically edit the product listings on the system.
Product Deliverer
When a Staff prepares an order for delivery, they will send a delivery request to the carrier. Postman will pick up the product and deliver it to the customer.

Operating Environment 
OE-1:The COW shall operate correctly with the following web browsers: Firefox versions 12 through 26; Google Chrome (all versions).
OE-2:The COW shall operate on a server running the current corporate-approved versions of Red Hat Linux and Apache HTTP Server.
OE-3:The COW shall permit user access from a VPN Internet connection, and by Android, IOS, and Windows smartphones and tablets.

Data Integrity, Retention, and Disposal
DIS-01:System Database will retain Individual Customer orders for 10 months following the delivery date.
DIS-02:The system shall retain data of promotion for one year following the promotion ended.
External Interface Requirements
 User Interfaces
UI-01:The system shall provide a help link from each displayed webpage to explain how to use that page.
UI-02:The webpages shall permit complete navigation and food item selection by using the keyboard alone, in addition to using mouse and keyboard combinations.

 Software Interfaces
SI-01:Store Inventory System
SI-011:The System shall transmit the quantities of product items ordered to the Store Inventory System through a programmatic interface.
SI-012:The System shall poll the Store Inventory System to determine whether a requested product item is available.
SI-013:When the Store Inventory System notifies the system that a specific product item is run out, the system shall hidden that product item in the website for the current date.
SI-02:Payroll System
The system shall communicate with the Payroll System through a programmatic interface for the following operations:

SI-021:To allow a customer to register and disable their account.
SI-022:To submit a payment request for a purchased order.
SI-023:To reverse all or part of a previous charge because a customer rejected a product or wasnâ€™t satisfied with quality of product and back the product to the store.
Hardware Interfaces
Our clientâ€™s hardware components must meet the minimum requirements of our system.
Communications Interfaces
CI-01:The system shall send an email (based on user account) to the customer to confirm acceptance of an order, online bill of the order. 
CI-02:The system shall send an email (based on user account) to the customer to report any problems with the product order or delivery.
Quality Attributes
Usability Requirements
USE-01:The system shall allow a customer to reorder the previous product ordered with a single interaction.
USE-02:85% of new users shall be able to successfully order a product without errors on their first try.
Performance Requirements
PR-01:The system shall accommodate a total of 100 users and a maximum of 40 concurrent users.
PR-02:95% of webpages generated by the system shall download completely within 10 seconds from the time the user requests the page over a 15Mbps or faster Internet connection.
PR-03:The system shall display confirmation messages to users within an average of 5 seconds and a maximum of 10seconds after the user submits information to the system.
Security Requirements
SEC-01:All network transactions that involve financial information or personally identifiable information shall be encrypted per MD5 encryption method.
SEC-02:Users shall be required to log on to the website for all operations except viewing information of products or promotions.
SEC-03:Only Managers can be permitted to work with product list.
SEC-04:The system shall permit customer to view only orders that they submitted.
Safety Requirements
SAF-01:The user shall be able to see a list of all ingredients in any product, with ingredients highlighted that are known to cause allergic reactions with children or some special people.
Availability Requirements
AVL-01:The order feature will available from 7:00AM to 8:00PM, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
Robustness Requirements
ROB-01:If the connection between the user and the website is broken prior to a new order being either confirmed or terminated, the system shall enable the user to recover an incomplete order and continue working on it.
